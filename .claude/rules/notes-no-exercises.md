# Notes: leave out exercises

When adding textbook content to the hoofdstuk notes files:

- Do NOT add "Oefenzinnen" / "Is alles duidelijk?" practice sentences.
- Do NOT add or reference "Opdracht N" exercises (no copies, no "zie boek" pointers).
- DO add "Struikelwoorden" (tricky words) boxes: they are explanations, not practice. Put them in their own "## 2. Struikelwoorden" section, right after "1. Nieuwe woorden" and before "3. Werkwoordvervoegingen" (as in hoofdstuk 1), with a "###" heading per word, its meanings and example sentences. Do not put them at the end of the file.
- Only add the explanation: rules, tables, and the example sentences that illustrate them.
